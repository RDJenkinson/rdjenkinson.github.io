I am a postdoctoral researcher in theoretical physics at the University of Manchester.  
Broadly, I am interested in how quantum information behaves in curved space‑times and other
relativistic settings, and in what current and near‑term quantum devices can tell us about
fundamental physics.

My current research touches topics such as:

- Qubit simulations of fundamental physics
- Causality in quantum field theory
- Quantum field theory in curved spacetime
- The Unruh effect
- Black holes and Hawking radiation

Beyond research, I enjoy explaining these ideas in accessible ways in talks and teaching, and I am
always keen to collaborate across quantum information, high‑energy theory, and related areas. If
you think our interests might overlap, feel free to reach out.
